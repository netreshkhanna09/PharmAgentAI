FDA PROMOTIONAL GUIDELINES — CITATION AND REFERENCE REQUIREMENTS
Source: FDA Guidance on Good Reprint Practices and Citation Standards
Version: 2024

RULE 5.1 — CITATION REQUIREMENT FOR CLINICAL CLAIMS
Every efficacy claim derived from clinical trial data must include a citation
to the source trial. The citation must include at minimum:
- The ClinicalTrials.gov NCT identifier, OR
- The published journal article reference (author, journal, year), OR
- The approved label section reference

RULE 5.2 — CITATION FORMAT
Citations in promotional materials must be:
- Accurate and complete
- Directly accessible to healthcare professionals
- Reproducible (the cited source must support exactly the claim made)
A claim citing a study that does not directly support it is a misleading citation.

RULE 5.3 — UNPUBLISHED DATA
Claims based on unpublished data or data on file must be labeled as such.
Promotional materials may not present data on file with the same weight
as peer-reviewed published evidence without disclosure.

RULE 5.4 — SUBGROUP ANALYSIS LABELING
Claims based on subgroup analyses of clinical trials must be labeled as
"post-hoc analysis" or "subgroup analysis" and may not be presented with
the same prominence as primary endpoint results.

RULE 5.5 — REQUIRED NCT CITATION FORMAT
When citing ClinicalTrials.gov, use the format: (NCTxxxxxxxx)
Example: "Pembrolizumab demonstrated improved OS in a Phase III trial (NCT04512345)"
The NCT number must match the trial that actually generated the cited data.
